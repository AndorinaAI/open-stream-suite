## 🚀 How to Import the Page in Bitfocus Companion

1. Open your **Bitfocus Companion** admin interface in your web browser (`http://localhost:8888` or `http://127.0.0.1:8888`).
2. Navigate to the **Import / Export** tab.
3. Under the **Import Page** section, browse and select:  
   [`open_stream_suite_companion_page.json`](./open_stream_suite_companion_page.json)
4. Choose the target page number on your surface (e.g., Page 1).
5. Click **Import**:
   * All button banks will be populated with contrasting colors, labels, and direct calls to `http://localhost:8080/api/deck/...`.
   * Works immediately on hardware surfaces, Elgato Stream Decks bridged through Companion, and the Companion mobile/tablet web UI.
