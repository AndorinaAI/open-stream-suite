Official installers and launchers have been organized into dedicated top-level platform directories for clarity and ease of use:

- 🐧 **Linux**: [../Linux/](../Linux/) (`install.sh`, `Iniciar Open Stream Suite.sh`, `update.sh`)
- 🪟 **Windows**: [../Windows/](../Windows/) (`install.bat`, `Iniciar Open Stream Suite.bat`, `update.bat`)
- 🍎 **macOS**: [../Mac/](../Mac/)

👉 For complete instructions and troubleshooting steps, read: **[INSTALLATION_AND_STARTUP.md](../app/docs/INSTALLATION_AND_STARTUP.md)** · [Español](../app/docs/INSTALACION_E_INICIO.md).

> [!NOTE]
> We develop primarily on **GNU/Linux**. While Windows and macOS installers are functional, we do not use those proprietary platforms day-to-day. We welcome your feedback, testing, and GitHub issue reports!
