# Translation policy (ES/EN)

- **Spanish is the canonical source.** When a guide changes, update the Spanish
  `.md` first.
- Short guides and operational references have full translations.
- Long guides may be **abridged** when the header says so and links to the
  Spanish original. An abridged translation is not presented as full parity.
- Screenshots and visual examples are shared; UI strings live in
  `app/languages/`.
- Before publishing, check that ES/EN links exist and the title states the
  translation status.
