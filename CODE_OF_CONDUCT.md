# Code of Conduct

**English** · [Español](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.es.md)

This project adopts the [Contributor Covenant v2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

## Our pledge
An open, respectful and harassment-free community for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability,
ethnicity, gender identity, experience level, nationality, appearance, religion or orientation.

## Expected behavior
- Kindness and respect
- Accepting constructive feedback
- Focusing on what is best for the community

## Unacceptable behavior
- Harassment, insults, personal attacks or discrimination
- Publishing others' private information
- Spam or other conduct inappropriate in a professional setting

## Enforcement
Report incidents through a [private advisory](https://github.com/AndorinaAI/open-stream-suite/security/advisories/new)
or by contacting the maintainer [@AndorinaAI](https://github.com/AndorinaAI). Reports are handled confidentially.
